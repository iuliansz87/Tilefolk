# Tilefolk Support

Welcome to Tilefolk Support.

For help, bug reports or questions, email **[iulian.szasz@gmail.com](mailto:iulian.szasz@gmail.com)**.

Please include your device model, iOS version, Tilefolk version and a short description of what happened. Screenshots can help, but never send passwords, verification codes or payment details.

## Saved progress

Tilefolk saves your puzzles and town on your device. Sign in with Apple to use account backup and online features.

When changing devices, sign in with the same Apple account and connect to the internet to sync your saved progress. Avoid deleting the app while troubleshooting, as locally saved guest progress may be lost.

## Daily puzzles and rankings

Daily puzzles reset at midnight UTC. Your local reset time may change with daylight saving time.

Complete five daily puzzles to qualify for ranked play. Your next daily puzzle begins your competitive participation in Sprout League.

Practice replays do not change your original daily result or leaderboard score.

## Coins and purchases

Apple processes all real-money purchases.

If purchased coins have not appeared, reconnect to the internet and reopen the coin packs section in Shop. If the issue continues, contact support with the purchase date and product name.

For refund requests, visit [Apple’s Report a Problem page](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/).

## Notifications

Manage optional reminders and friend-request alerts in **Profile → Settings → Notifications**.

You can also change notification permissions in your device’s Settings app.

## Account deletion

To delete your Tilefolk account, open **Profile → Settings → Delete account** and follow the confirmation steps.

Deletion requires an internet connection and may require you to authenticate again. Deleting your Tilefolk account does not delete your Apple account or automatically refund purchases.

## Privacy and community concerns

For privacy requests, inappropriate player names or concerns about another player, email **[iulian.szasz@gmail.com](mailto:iulian.szasz@gmail.com)**. Include the player’s visible name or username where possible.
