# Tilefolk Privacy Policy

**Last updated 17 November 2026**

Iulian Szasz, based in Prague, Czechia, is the operator and data controller for Tilefolk.

This policy explains how we handle information when you use Tilefolk.

For privacy questions or requests, contact **[iulian.szasz@gmail.com](mailto:iulian.szasz@gmail.com)**.

## Information stored on your device

Tilefolk stores game progress, puzzle saves, town layouts, inventory, coin balances and preferences on your device.

Practice puzzles and town editing can be used without an online account. Account session credentials are stored using the device’s Keychain.

## Account information

If you sign in with Apple, we receive an account identifier and the information Apple makes available, which may include your name and email address. If you choose Hide My Email, Apple may provide a private relay address.

We also store your Tilefolk profile information, including your display name, username and selected avatar.

We use this information to authenticate your account, provide online features and back up your progress.

## Gameplay and social information

Online features process information such as:

- Puzzle attempts, moves, scores and completion dates.
- League participation and standings.
- Saved progress, achievements, inventory and town layouts.
- Friend connections and requests.
- Town likes, activity notifications and notification read status.

Tilefolk does not access your device’s address book to find friends.

## Information visible to other players

Your public display name, username, selected avatar, competitive scores and league information may be visible to other players.

Completed competitive boards may be available for viewing after the daily challenge closes.

When you sign in and your public profile syncs, other signed-in players can also see your town name and layout, progress statistics, and the badges you choose to pin. Tilefolk updates these profile details automatically while you are signed in.

Your email address and private account backup are not displayed on public profiles.

## Purchases

Apple handles payment processing. Tilefolk does not receive your payment card details.

We process purchase information, including product identifiers, transaction identifiers, purchase verification information and delivery records, to deliver coin packs and prevent duplicate delivery.

Account-linked coin credits and spending records may be stored to synchronize and reconcile your balance.

## Notifications and Live Activities

Daily reminders are scheduled locally on your device.

If you enable social alerts, we store an Apple Push Notification service token and related registration information linked to your account to deliver friend and town-like alerts. Optional Daily countdowns use Live Activity tokens so the countdown can start and end on your device.

You can manage these features in Tilefolk and your device settings. Turning social alerts off in Tilefolk removes the online registration when the app connects.

## Online service logs

When you use online features, Supabase records technical request information, including IP addresses and, for some requests, an IP-derived country code. The logs help operate, secure, and troubleshoot Tilefolk. Country codes used for these logs come from network requests.

## Support communications

If you contact us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. You can choose to share a wallet diagnostic with support. It contains wallet and purchase-delivery details that help investigate the issue.

## How we use information

We use information to:

- Provide and save gameplay.
- Authenticate accounts and synchronize progress.
- Validate competitive results and operate rankings.
- Deliver purchases and prevent duplicate rewards or transactions.
- Provide friends, town sharing and requested notifications.
- Respond to support requests and maintain service security.

We do not use the information described in this policy for cross-app advertising tracking.

## Service providers

Tilefolk uses Apple services for features such as Sign in with Apple, purchases, Game Center and push notification delivery.

We use Supabase for online authentication, database storage and backend services.

These providers process information needed to deliver their services. Their privacy information is available at:

- [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)
- [Supabase Privacy Policy](https://supabase.com/privacy)

## Storage locations and international transfers

Tilefolk’s primary Supabase database and authentication service are hosted in Ireland. Apple and Supabase may process some information in other countries to deliver their services. Supabase’s [Data Processing Addendum](https://supabase.com/legal/customer-resources/data-processing-addendum) describes the safeguards for covered transfers, including Standard Contractual Clauses where applicable.

## Data retention

We retain account and gameplay information while your account remains active so that we can provide your saved progress and online features.

After account deletion, associated account data is removed from our live systems. Temporary provider recovery copies, where present, expire under the provider’s retention settings. Supabase’s API and database logs are currently retained for one day and may remain until that period expires.

We keep support correspondence as long as needed to resolve the request and handle related follow-up. We retain information for longer where a legal obligation requires it.

## Account deletion and your choices

You can request account deletion through **Profile → Settings → Delete account**.

Deletion removes your live Tilefolk account and associated profile, backup, social connections, ranked results and related account records. It also clears game data on the device where you complete deletion.

Copies stored on other offline devices are not remotely erased. Uninstalling Tilefolk alone does not delete your online account.

Account deletion does not delete your Apple account, remove records Apple retains independently or automatically refund purchases.

You can also contact us to request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information.

## Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, restrict or object to certain processing, and withdraw consent where processing relies on it.

Where applicable, you may also complain to your local data protection authority.

For users in the European Economic Area or United Kingdom, we process the information needed to provide the account services and purchases you request on the basis of performing our agreement with you. We rely on our legitimate interests to keep Tilefolk secure, prevent misuse, troubleshoot service issues, and handle support. Where processing relies on consent, you can withdraw it through the relevant setting or by contacting us. We keep information required by law on the basis of that legal obligation.

Contact **[iulian.szasz@gmail.com](mailto:iulian.szasz@gmail.com)** to make a request. We may need to verify your identity before acting on it.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Tilefolk changes. The date above identifies the latest revision. Where required, we will provide notice of material changes.
