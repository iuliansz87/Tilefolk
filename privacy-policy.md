## Information stored on your device

Tilefolk stores game progress, puzzle saves, town layouts, inventory, coin balances and preferences on your device.

Practice puzzles and town editing can be used without an online account. Account session credentials are stored using the device’s Keychain.

## Account information

If you sign in with Apple, we receive an account identifier and the information Apple makes available, which may include your name and email address. If you choose Hide My Email, Apple may provide a private relay address.

We also store your Tilefolk profile information, including your display name, username and selected avatar.

We use this information to authenticate your account, provide online features and back up your progress.

## Gameplay and social information

Online features process information such as:

- Puzzle attempts, moves, scores and completion dates.
- League participation and standings.
- Saved progress, achievements, inventory and town layouts.
- Friend connections and requests.
- Town likes, activity notifications and notification read status.

Tilefolk does not access your device’s address book to find friends.

## Information visible to other players

Your public display name, username, selected avatar, competitive scores and league information may be visible to other players.

Completed competitive boards may be available for viewing after the daily challenge closes.

If you enable profile sharing, other players may also see your shared town, progress statistics and selected achievement badges.

Your email address and private account backup are not displayed on public profiles.

## Purchases

Apple handles payment processing. Tilefolk does not receive your payment card details.

We process purchase information, including product identifiers, transaction identifiers, purchase verification information and delivery records, to deliver coin packs and prevent duplicate delivery.

Account-linked coin credits and spending records may be stored to synchronize and reconcile your balance.

## Notifications and Live Activities

Daily reminders are scheduled locally on your device.

If you enable friend-request push notifications, we store an Apple Push Notification service token and related registration information linked to your account so we can deliver those alerts.

Where enabled, Live Activities display the daily puzzle countdown and related status.

You can manage these features in Tilefolk and your device settings.

## Support communications

If you contact us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use it to respond to your request and investigate the issue.

## How we use information

We use information to:

- Provide and save gameplay.
- Authenticate accounts and synchronize progress.
- Validate competitive results and operate rankings.
- Deliver purchases and prevent duplicate rewards or transactions.
- Provide friends, town sharing and requested notifications.
- Respond to support requests and maintain service security.

We do not use the information described in this policy for cross-app advertising tracking.

## Service providers

## Account deletion and your choices

You can request account deletion through **Profile → Settings → Delete account**.

Deletion removes your live Tilefolk account and associated profile, backup, social connections, ranked results and related account records. It also clears game data on the device where you complete deletion.

Copies stored on other offline devices are not remotely erased. Uninstalling Tilefolk alone does not delete your online account.

Account deletion does not delete your Apple account, remove records Apple retains independently or automatically refund purchases.

You can also contact us to request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information.
